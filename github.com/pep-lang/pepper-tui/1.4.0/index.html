tui

tui 1.4.0

Full-screen terminal apps in the style of Ratatui: widgets, layout and diffed rendering over pepper-term

Pages

  • tui: Full-screen terminal apps in the style of Ratatui: immediate-mode drawing, layout by constraints, styled text, and widgets, over pepper-term.
  • barchart.pep: BarChart: labeled vertical bars.
  • block.pep: Block: a border with a title around another widget, and the area inside it.
  • buffer.pep: The screen as data​: a Buffer of Cells, which widgets draw into and which Terminal compares with the last frame's to write only what changed.
  • events.pep: Input events: key presses, mouse reports and pastes from pepper-term's InputReader, and resizes of the terminal.
  • gauge.pep: Gauge: a bar filled to a ratio, with a label.
  • geometry.pep: Rectangles of cells: Rect, the area a widget draws in.
  • layout.pep: Layout: Layout splits a Rect into a row or a column of smaller ones, sized by Constraints.
  • list.pep: List: items one under another, one of them selected, scrolled to keep the selection in view; ListState holds the selection and the scroll.
  • paragraph.pep: Paragraph: text in an area, cut at its edge or wrapped at word boundaries, and scrolled.
  • restore.pep: Leaving the full-screen state however the program ends, private to the module: at exit, and on the signals that end it, a panic's SIGABRT included.
  • scrollbar.pep: Scrollbar: a track along one edge of an area, with a thumb showing where the view is; ScrollbarState says how long the content is and where the view sits.
  • sparkline.pep: Sparkline: a row of tiny bars, one column per value.
  • style.pep: Styles that layer: a Style sets only some of a cell's colors and modifiers, and patch lays one style over another, so a widget's style, a line's and a span's combine as Ratatui's do.
  • table.pep: Table: rows of cells in columns sized by constraints, with a header and a selected row, scrolled like a List.
  • tabs.pep: Tabs: a row of titles with one selected.
  • terminal.pep: Drawing a frame at a time: Terminal keeps the last frame's Buffer, has the app draw the next one into a Frame, and sends its backend only the cells that changed.
  • text.pep: Styled text: a Span is a run of text in one style, a Line a row of spans, and a Text a stack of lines.
  • text_input.pep: TextInput: an editable field of text, on one row or wrapped over many, with a cursor, a selection and pastes; TextInputState holds the text and the cursor from frame to frame.
  • widget.pep: The widget traits: a Widget draws itself into a buffer, and a StatefulWidget draws with state the app keeps between frames, such as a list's selection.
  • width.pep: Display width: how many terminal columns a character or a string takes.

Dependencies

  • term
  • unicode
